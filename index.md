### About Me
Shalini Singh is a multi hyphenate writer and multimedia artist born in Gaya, Bihar, and now based in Orlando, Florida. A former practicing lawyer and educator turned rhetorician, she works across lyric essay, text-image collage, assemblages. Her practice is trying to decenter men from her life by way of displacement, intimacy,  racism, chronic illness and the immigrant woman’s body of shame and guilt.

Her writing has appeared in The Nation, Outlook India, Hayden’s Ferry Review, Sonora Review, Berkeley Poetry Review, Southeast Review, Puerto del Sol, and Hobart. Her work has been nominated for Best American Essays 2025 and the 20 Under 30 Best South Asian Fiction Writers. She is the author of the prose poetry book Confessions of a Turophile. She serves on the editorial team of DIAGRAM and has an extensive editorial experience. 

Shalini holds an MFA in Creative Writing & Environment from Iowa State University and law degrees (JD equivalent amd LLM) from GNLU, India. She is a PhD student in Texts & Technology at the University of Central Florida, and has taught writing, rhetoric, and technical writing for computing at Florida International University.
